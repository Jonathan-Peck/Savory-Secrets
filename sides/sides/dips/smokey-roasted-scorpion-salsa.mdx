## Ingredients
• 1/2 to 1 Trinidad Scorpion pepper (stemmed and minced microscopic—remember your gloves!)
• 6 medium Roma tomatoes (cut in half)
• 1 large white onion (cut into thick wedges)
• 4 large garlic cloves (peel left on for roasting, removed before blending)
• 1/2 cup rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar (essential for the 1-month shelf life)
• 1/4 cup fresh lime juice
• 1.5 teaspoons salt
• 1 teaspoon ground cumin
• 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika (amplifies the smoky charcoal flavor)

## Instructions 
<Tabs>
  <TabItem value="roasted" label="Roasted">
1. Char the Veggies (The Smoke Factor)
Turn your oven broiler to HIGH and move the oven rack to the top slot. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil. Place the halved tomatoes (skin side up), onion wedges, 
and unpeeled garlic cloves on the tray. Broil for 6 to 8 minutes until the tomato skins are black and blistered and the onions have dark, charred edges.
<br/>
2. Handle the Scorpion Pepper
While the veggies roast, put on your protective gloves. Slice your Trinidad Scorpion pepper open. Scrape out the seeds and inner white membrane if you want pure heat 
without a bitter edge. Mince a quarter, a half, or the whole pepper into microscopic pieces.
<br/>
3. Blend it Up
Squeeze the roasted garlic out of their skins. Throw the warm tomatoes, onions, and garlic into a blender or food processor. Add your minced Scorpion pepper, vinegar, 
lime juice, salt, cumin, and smoked paprika. Pulse 5 to 7 times. You want a cohesive, slightly chunky consistency—don't over-process into a watery juice.
<br/>
4. The 1-Month Preservation Simmer
Pour the blended salsa into a saucepan and bring it to a rolling boil over medium-high heat. Immediately lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Let it cook uncovered for 15 to 20 minutes, 
stirring occasionally. This crucial step drives off excess water, concentrates the smoky flavor, fuses the acids, and destroys any natural bacteria to ensure it holds up in the fridge.
<br/>
5. Jar and Cool
Ladle the hot salsa directly into a meticulously clean, warm mason jar, leaving about a half-inch of space at the top. Let the jar sit open on your kitchen counter until it 
reaches room temperature. Wipe the rim clean, screw the lid on tightly, and store it in the coldest part of your refrigerator (the back of the main shelf, not the door).
